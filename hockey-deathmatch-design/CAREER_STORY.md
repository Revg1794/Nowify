# RHL — Career Story: "Death Waiver" (Brainstorm v0.1)

Told entirely through the **Meridian broadcast** — pre-game shows, interviews, news tickers, jumbotron, leaked footage, texts.
No cinematic cutscenes. Playable as a **skater or a goalie** (same story, different mentor and beats).

## 2. Core Systems

### Two Reputation Meters (drive choices and endings)
| Meter | Raised by | Lowered by |
|---|---|---|
| **Network Favor** | Taking network deals, throwing games, hyping sponsors, brutal highlight plays | Defying Holloway, trash-talking the network, refusing deals |
| **Locker Room Loyalty** | Protecting teammates, staying with the Dockrats, revenge for teammates, honesty in interviews | Selling out teammates, leaving for the High Rollers, throwing games |

### Ratings (currency)
- Earned by exciting, brutal play. Spent on gear, medical care, training, and contract leverage.
- Low ratings → pay cuts → "retirement" risk (story threat).

### Rivals
- Hurting a star player starts a rivalry — they hunt you in later games and appear in interviews.
- Rivals can become allies through story choices.

### Persistent Body
- Scars, missing teeth, broken bones carry across seasons. Bad injuries can force you to miss games.

### Death in Career
- **Standard:** "death" becomes a career-threatening injury — you miss games and lose ratings.
- **Permadeath (option):** if you die, play on as **Next of Kin** — your younger sibling signs a death waiver to finish what you started (keeps story progress, new player).

## 4. Story Outline

### Act 1 — Rookie Waiver (Season 1)
1. **Open:** Black screen, a contract, a pen. You sign the death waiver. Tasha Vane: "Meet the newest Dockrat — odds of surviving the season: 31%."
2. **Debut** at Pier 13 vs Detroit. Frankie (or Tommy) takes you under their wing.
3. **Rival born:** Ace Delacroix humiliates you on broadcast. Interview choice sets your public persona.
4. **The Offer:** a Meridian rep (texts) offers money to lose a game. First big choice.
5. **Leaked footage:** a "hidden camera" locker room segment airs — Frankie arguing with Holloway's people.
6. **Playoffs vs Las Vegas at The Jackpot:** refs look away while Ace (and his teammates) target Frankie/Tommy.
7. **End of Act 1 — "The Accident" (preventable):**
   - **Mentor dies (default):** your mentor is killed on live ice. The network calls it an accident. Dale cuts to commercial.
     You're named captain (skater) or starting goalie (goalie path).
   - **Mentor saved:** if you protect your mentor during the playoff game (keep them above a health threshold — block hits,
     fight their attackers, take the hits yourself — **and** win the game), they survive, badly injured.
     - Holloway's people "retire" a different Dockrat instead (still a gut punch).
     - The mentor is forced off the ice but becomes an **off-ice ally** in Acts 2–3 (insider knowledge, extra texts/segments).
     - Unlocks bonus content: mentor joins Knuckles' plan; in the **Live Feed** and **Burn It Down** endings the mentor gets a final moment.
     - You're still named captain / starting goalie (mentor hands it to you).

### Act 2 — Ratings War (Season 2)
1. **The face of the Dockrats.** Sponsors (VoltRush, PainAway) come calling — take deals or refuse.
2. **Knuckles' code:** Knuckles drops hints on air ("Some players retire early in this league, kid…").
3. **The Retirement List:** Tasha finds a pattern — players whose ratings dropped died in "accidents."
4. **The Wardens game:** Malachi Cross offers info in exchange for help winning his pardon. Ally or betray.
5. **The Trade Offer:** Holloway offers you a spot on the **High Rollers** — fame, money, safety. Biggest choice in the game.
   - Accept → Act 3 from inside the network (High Rollers path).
   - Refuse → Act 3 as the underdog (Dockrats path).
6. **Ace's fall:** Ace's ratings dip — he's next on the list. He comes to you scared. Rival → ally or let him burn.
7. **End of Act 2:** Tasha is pulled off the air. Knuckles gives you a flash drive: the list, the payments, the rigged calls.

### Act 3 — Prime Time (Season 3)
1. **Meridian rebrands the league** around you — your face on the jumbotron everywhere.
2. **Holloway's ultimatum:** lose the Meridian Cup Final live, or your teammates (or family) pay.
3. **Gauntlet of hazards:** every arena's Network Event is turned up against you — the network is trying to kill you on TV.
4. **The Meridian Cup Final** at the **Meridian Dome** vs **Cole Mercer and the Meridian All-Stars** (goalie path: vs "The Firewall" in the opposite net).

## 5. Endings

| Ending | How you get it | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **The Product** | High Network Favor, accept Holloway's deal, throw the Final | You become the new face of Meridian. Final shot: your face on the jumbotron, smiling. Next season, someone else signs a death waiver. (Dark ending.) |
| **Live Feed** | High Locker Room Loyalty, win the Final, protected Tasha/Knuckles | During the Final, Knuckles hijacks the broadcast and airs the evidence live. You win; Holloway is arrested on his own network. |
| **Final Whistle** | Win the Final while badly injured / low health | You score the winner and collapse on the ice. The crowd chants your number. The league is never the same. (Bittersweet.) |
| **Burn It Down** (secret) | Max Hype + Loyalty, Malachi and Ace as allies | The players turn on the network in the Final — riot in the Meridian Dome, the broadcast cuts to static. |

## 6. Broadcast Presentation (how story is told)

> Full segment design, scripts, and production plan: `CAREER_BROADCAST.md`.

| Segment | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Redline Tonight** | Pre-game show — Dale and Knuckles preview games, react to story events |
| **Rinkside with Tasha Vane** | Post-game interview with dialogue choices (Humble / Trash Talk / Defy Network) |
| **Meridian News Ticker** | Scrolling headlines between games (story hints, league events, injuries, deaths) |
| **Commissioner's Address** | Holloway's weekly message to the league (gets more threatening) |
| **Leaked Footage** | Grainy "hidden camera" clips that reveal the conspiracy |
| **Phone / Texts** | Messages from Meridian reps, teammates, Tasha, unknown numbers |
| **Tabloid / Newspaper screens** | Still images + headlines after big moments |
| **Year in Blood** | End-of-season highlight reel built automatically from **your own** replays |

- **Voices:** local TTS (ComfyUI) for Dale, Knuckles, Tasha, Holloway, players; or record our own.
- **Visuals:** in-engine broadcast desk set, MetaHuman characters, stills generated in ComfyUI, UE UMG for TV graphics.
