## Shortlist (Claude's picks)
1. **Blood Line** — hockey's red line + violence + lineage (career/dynasty mode)
2. **Kill Shift** — "shift" is a hockey term; sounds like a TV show
3. **Red Ice** — simple, visual, instantly tells you what the game is
4. **Sudden Death** — perfect but likely heavily used / trademarked
5. **Five for Fighting** — hockey fans get it instantly (note: also a band name)
