# RHL — League, Network & Teams (Brainstorm v0.1)

All names here are fictional. Avoid real team names, logos, colors schemes that copy NHL/AHL clubs, and real companies.

---

## The World

Near future (~2040s), grounded — no sci-fi tech. Economic collapse, privatized cities, legal gray zones.
Traditional pro sports went bankrupt. A media conglomerate bought the leftover arenas and players' contracts
and relaunched hockey as the **Redline Hockey League**: real hockey, real violence, legal by special waiver.
Players sign "death waivers." Families get paid if they die on the ice. Ratings are everything.

---

## The Network: **MERIDIAN**

Bland, corporate, smiling — which makes it scarier.
- **Network:** Meridian Media Group
- **Flagship show:** *Redline Live* (Friday night prime time)
- **Slogan:** "Meridian. We bring you closer."
- **Alt names considered:** OmniCast, Apex Broadcasting, The Feed

### Key Characters
| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| **Commissioner Vance Holloway** | Smooth, polished face of the league. Rigs outcomes behind the scenes. Final career-mode antagonist. |
| **Dale "The Voice" Marrow** | Play-by-play announcer. Over-the-top hype man, sponsor plugs mid-kill. |
| **Rene "Knuckles" Dupree** | Color commentator. Former RHL enforcer, missing teeth, brutally honest, quietly hates the network. |
| **Tasha Vane** | Rinkside reporter. Interviews players seconds after kills. |
| **The Medics** | Meridian's "medical team." Slow to arrive unless you're a ratings star. |

## Launch Teams (8)

Each team = a playstyle + a home arena with its own hazard. Arenas are the main level-design variety.

| # | Team | Identity / Playstyle | Home Arena & Hazard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Detroit Ironworkers** | Blue-collar enforcers. Heavy hitters, slow. | **The Plant** — abandoned auto factory. Overhead conveyor hooks swing through the rink. |
| 2 | **Gary Slagdogs** | Dirty players, burn damage, bleed tactics. | **Mill No. 9** — steel mill. Molten-slag glow behind the glass; boards get scorching hot. |
| 3 | **Winnipeg Wardens** | Prison team — inmates play for sentence reductions. Pure violence, no defense. | **The Yard** — prison rink. Guards enter with batons if a fight runs long; searchlights. |
| 4 | **Las Vegas High Rollers** | Network darlings. Skilled, flashy, refs suspiciously favor them. | **The Jackpot** — casino arena. Crowd betting changes item drops; slot-machine jumbotron. |
| 5 | **New Orleans Drowned** | Fast, slippery, puck-skill team. | **The Barge** — rink on a floating barge. Thin ice in spots, water underneath. |
| 6 | **Anchorage Butchers** | Bruisers sponsored by a meatpacker. Hooks and blades. | **Kold Kut Arena** — partially outdoor. Blizzards cut visibility; meat hooks on the walls. |
| 7 | **Bayonne Dockrats** | Scrappy underdogs, tricks and traps. | **Pier 13** — shipping-container arena. Crane drops containers/loot onto the ice. |
| 8 | **Sudbury Deep** | Silent, disciplined miners. Defensive, punishing. | **The Shaft** — underground rink in a mine. Low light, headlamps, cave-in debris. |

## Career Mode Hook

You're a nobody signed to the **Bayonne Dockrats** (worst team in the league) on a death waiver.
Climb through ratings, sponsors, and survival. Discover Holloway rigs games and "retires" players who stop drawing ratings.
Final season: beat the Meridian All-Stars live on *Redline Live* — and decide whether to play the network's game or burn it down.
