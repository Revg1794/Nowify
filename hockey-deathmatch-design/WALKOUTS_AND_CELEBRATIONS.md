# RHL — Walkouts & Celebrations (Brainstorm v0.1)

Personality on the ice. Every star gets a **walkout**, **goal/save celebrations**, a **kill celebration**, and a **fight-win** moment.
Custom players pick from a generic library. Builds on `STAR_PLAYERS.md`.

---

## 1. When They Play

| Moment | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Team entrance** (pre-game) | 6–10 s | Each team's unique arena entrance |
| **Star walkout** (pre-game) | 3–6 s | Spotlight on 1–2 stars per team (announcer intro) |
| **Goal celebration** | 2–4 s | During the stoppage after a goal |
| **Kill celebration** | 1–2 s | **On the fly** — upper-body taunt while skating (play doesn't stop) |
| **Fight win** | 2–3 s | After the fight ends |
| **Big save / goalie celebration** | 1–2 s | On the fly (goalie stays ready) or during stoppage after a freeze |
| **Win / Three Stars** | 3–5 s | Post-game: winning team + Three Stars salute |

### Rules
- Everything is **skippable** (any button) after the first second.
- **Settings:** Walkouts & Intros: Full / Short (stars only) / Off · Celebrations: On / Quick / Off.
- **Taunt risk:** celebrating near opponents can get you hit (you're vulnerable for the first 0.5 s of an on-the-fly celly).
- Celebrations give a small **Hype bump** (+2), more for star signatures (+4).
- Camera: broadcast-style cuts (handheld rinkside cam, low angle, jumbotron cut-ins).

## 8. Production ($0)
- **Base motions:** Mixamo + UE Game Animation Sample (skating, gestures, taunts, dances).
- **Custom celebrations:** built with **Control Rig** keyframing in Unreal (Claude can script/adjust poses) + Blender for props (time card, rubber rat, flare, chips, dice, chains, headlamp).
- **On-the-fly kill/save celebrations:** upper-body layered animations (skating continues underneath).
- Entrance sets reuse arena kit pieces (gates, container, cage, lift) + Niagara (steam, sparks, confetti, frost, fog).
- Simplify anything too complex to animate well — readability over complexity.
