# RHL — Game Modes (Brainstorm v0.1)

Every mode is framed as a **Meridian broadcast** (show name, intro, announcers) to keep the world consistent.

---

## 1. Story / Single-Player

### Career: "Death Waiver" (flagship single-player)
- Create a custom player → sign a death waiver with the **Bayonne Dockrats**.
- Seasons of matches + off-ice scenes: locker room, press conferences with Tasha Vane, sponsor deals, Holloway meetings.
- **Ratings = currency.** Brutal, exciting play earns sponsors, gear, fame. Boring wins cut your pay.
- **Choices:** take the network's scripted outcomes ("lose this game, we'll pay you") or defy them.
- **Persistent body:** scars, injuries, missing teeth carry over. Bad injuries can end your career early.
- Rivalries with the star players form from your actions (injure Ace Delacroix → he hunts you).
- Ending: beat the Meridian All-Stars on *Redline Live*. Multiple endings (join the network / expose Holloway / die on the ice).

### Franchise: "Ownership"
- Run a team (any of the 8 or a custom expansion team) as owner/GM over many seasons.
- Trades, free agency, draft from prison leagues / mill leagues / street leagues.
- **Permadeath roster:** dead players are gone. Pay death-waiver settlements to their families (budget hit).
- Medical budget, arena upgrades (add hazards), sponsor contracts, ticket pricing vs. riot risk.
- Sim or play each game.

### Survival: "Last Shift"
- You + AI teammates (or co-op) vs. endless waves of enemy teams.
- Can't respawn; injuries stack. Score with goals and kills; buy items between periods.
- Leaderboards.

### Challenges: "Highlight Reel"
- Short scripted scenarios: "Down 2 with 60 seconds left," "Score with a broken arm," "Win with only your goalie alive."
- Medals, unlocks (gear, celebrations, logo pieces).

---

## 2. Quick Play / Local

### Exhibition
- Any teams (including custom), 3v3 or 5v5, any arena, settings: item frequency (Classic / Chaos / Off), hazards on/off, period length, permadeath in-match.

### Local Multiplayer (couch)
- Up to 4 players (2v2 split controllers on one team or versus). Classic couch arcade hockey energy.

### Practice: "The Pit"
- Training rink: skating, shooting, checking, fights, every item. Tutorial lives here.

---

## 3. Online

### Ranked: "Prime Time"
- 3v3 standard, competitive item rules (low item rate, no catch-up logic), all star players + custom players with normalized stats.
- Seasons, ranks (e.g. Rookie → Grinder → Enforcer → Headliner → Prime Time).

### Casual / Quick Match
- 3v3 / 5v5, full Chaos items allowed.

### Online Career: "The League" (long-term mode)
- Your custom player only. Join other players as a team (6 humans + goalie).
- Persistent player progression and cosmetics.
- Friends-and-clubs: create a club (custom team), play scheduled club matches.

### Co-op vs AI
- Team up against AI star teams. Good for new players.

---

## 4. Party / Variant Modes (rotating, limited-time)

| Mode | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Last Team Standing** | No goals count — eliminate the whole opposing team (pure deathmatch). |
| **One Puck, One Life** | No respawns. Score or kill everyone. |
| **Hot Potato** | The puck is a time bomb; you must score or pass before it detonates. |
| **Blackout** | Arena lights off the whole game; only flares, headlamps, and muzzle-flash of slapshots. |
| **King of the Crease** | Hold the crease to score points over time. |
| **Juggernaut** | One heavily armored player vs. everyone. Whoever kills them becomes the Juggernaut. |
| **Bounty** | Network puts a bounty on the top scorer; killing them gives your team huge Hype. |
| **Ice Breaker** | Ice shrinks over time as it breaks away — battle-royale style. |
| **Goalie Gauntlet** | Skaters take turns trying to score on a goalie loaded with crease traps. |
| **Playoffs Bracket** | Tournament with permadeath carried through the bracket. |

---

## 5. Special Formats

- **Penalty Shootout of Death** — tied games end in shootouts; goalie can attack the shooter.
- **Fight Night** — standalone fight mini-game mode (1v1 brawl on the ice). Good for quick sessions & testing the fight system.
- **Spectator / Broadcast Mode** — watch AI or friends' games with full Meridian presentation; replay editor for highlight clips (shareable — good for marketing/streamers).

---

## 6. DECISIONS (Oct 2026)

- **Platform:** PC only. **No online.** No console release planned.
- **Launch modes:** Career, Franchise, Challenges, Penalty Shootout of Death, Broadcast/Replay mode.
- **Core supporting modes:** Exhibition (incl. local couch multiplayer), Practice / tutorial.
- **Career presentation:** fully voiced cutscenes (to confirm — see below).
- **Cut / not planned:** all online modes (Ranked, Quick Match, The League, online co-op), crossplay.
- **Maybe later (offline):** Survival, Fight Night, Party modes as local/exhibition variants.

## 7. Build Order

| Phase | Modes |
|---|---|
| **Vertical slice** | Exhibition (3v3, one arena), Practice |
| **Alpha** | Penalty Shootout of Death, Challenges, Broadcast/Replay mode |
| **Beta** | Franchise, Career (story + voiced cutscenes) |
| **Post-launch (optional)** | Survival, Fight Night, Party variants |

No online = no servers, netcode, anti-cheat, or moderation. Big savings — that budget goes to Career presentation and art quality.
