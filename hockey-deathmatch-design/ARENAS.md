# RHL — Arenas (Brainstorm v0.1)

Every arena = **standard rink core + themed shell + hazards + one big network event**.
Hazards can be toggled per match (Exhibition settings), so any arena can be played "clean."

---

## Shared Rules

- **Rink core:** real NHL dimensions — 200 ft × 85 ft (≈ 61 m × 26 m), boards ~42 in, glass above. Same gameplay space everywhere so skating/shooting feel is consistent.
- **What changes per arena:** boards material, glass (or no glass/cage/chain-link), lighting, ice condition, crowd, hazards, item delivery style, ambient audio, jumbotron/broadcast graphics.
- **Hazard types:**
  - **Passive** — always on (hot boards, thin ice zones, low light)
  - **Triggered** — timed or random (crane drop, conveyor hook swing)
  - **Network Event** — fires at **Crowd Hype Tier 3**, the arena's signature moment
- **Readability rule:** every hazard is telegraphed (sound + light + announcer) at least 2 seconds before it hits.
- **Weather & time of day (all arenas):** selectable per match — Day / Dusk / Night; Clear / Rain / Snow / Fog / Storm (indoor arenas get matching effects: leaks, steam, power flickers). Each arena still has a **signature default** mood.
- **Ice damage:** blood, skate marks, cracks, and scorch marks accumulate through the game and reset between periods (Zamboni).

## The Arenas

### 1. The Plant — Detroit Ironworkers
- **Setting:** Gutted auto assembly plant. Rink built on the factory floor; rusted car frames hang from the ceiling line.
- **Look:** Sodium-orange work lights, rust, oil stains, steel beams, sparks.
- **Boards / glass:** Riveted steel boards, chain-link fencing instead of glass.
- **Passive:** Chain-link boards — players slammed into them get cut.
- **Triggered:** **Conveyor hooks** sweep across the ice on a track every ~90 s.
- **Network Event — "Shift Change":** Factory whistle blows, robotic welding arms along the boards spark to life; anyone checked into them takes heavy damage.
- **Crowd:** Laid-off workers in hard hats, banging wrenches on the fence.
- **Item delivery:** Items ride in on the conveyor hooks.

### 2. Mill No. 9 — Gary Slagdogs
- **Setting:** Active steel mill. Ladles of molten steel pour behind the glass.
- **Look:** Deep orange glow, heat haze, smoke, black soot.
- **Boards / glass:** Heat-scorched steel, cracked heat-resistant glass.
- **Passive:** **Hot boards** — sections glow red and burn on contact (cycle around the rink).
- **Triggered:** Slag spill — a corner of the ice melts to slush for 20 s.
- **Network Event — "The Pour":** A ladle tips; a river of molten glow runs under a strip of ice, splitting the rink. Cross it and burn.
- **Crowd:** Mill workers in fire suits, flare-lit stands.
- **Item delivery:** Dropped from an overhead crane bucket.

### 3. The Yard — Winnipeg Wardens
- **Setting:** Outdoor prison yard rink. Razor wire, guard towers, floodlights, snow falling.
- **Look:** Cold blue-white floodlights, sweeping searchlights, concrete walls.
- **Boards / glass:** Concrete barriers + chain-link topped with razor wire.
- **Passive:** **Searchlights** — players caught in the light are marked (easier to target).
- **Triggered:** SafeHaven guards enter if a fight lasts too long and baton both fighters.
- **Network Event — "Lockdown":** Sirens; cage walls drop, splitting the rink into thirds for 30 s. Puck can pass through bars, players can't.
- **Crowd:** Inmates behind fences, guards with shotguns on catwalks. No home-team cheering — just chanting and banging.
- **Item delivery:** Contraband thrown over the wall by inmates.

### 4. The Jackpot — Las Vegas High Rollers
- **Setting:** Casino mega-arena. Neon, gold trim, slot machines in the concourse.
- **Look:** Bright, glossy, saturated colors — the cleanest arena (that's the satire).
- **Boards / glass:** Pristine white boards covered in sponsor ads; spotless glass.
- **Passive:** **Rigged refs** — penalties against the High Rollers are called less often (exhibition toggle).
- **Triggered:** **The Wheel** — jumbotron spins a prize wheel; result changes the next item drop (good or cursed).
- **Network Event — "Jackpot":** Confetti cannons, item rain of 6–8 random items across the ice. Free-for-all.
- **Crowd:** Rich bettors in suits, bottle service, showgirls. Crowd odds displayed live on screens.
- **Item delivery:** Gold briefcases lowered from the ceiling.

### 5. The Barge — New Orleans Drowned
- **Setting:** Floating rink on a rusted barge in a flooded city. Half-sunken buildings around it.
- **Look:** Humid night, fog, string lights, neon reflections on dark water, moss.
- **Boards / glass:** Waterlogged wood boards, patched with sheet metal; low rail in spots.
- **Passive:** **Thin ice** — dark patches; heavy hits on them crack the ice.
- **Triggered:** Barge sways — ice tilts slightly, puck slides one direction for a few seconds.
- **Network Event — "Floodgate":** A section of ice breaks open; players knocked in fall through and are **out for the rest of their shift** (respawn on next line change). No drowning/swimming.
- **Crowd:** Locals on surrounding boats and rooftops, brass band.
- **Item delivery:** Floats up from under the ice, frozen in place.

### 6. Kold Kut Arena — Anchorage Butchers
- **Setting:** Half-indoor arena attached to a meatpacking plant. One end is open to the outside.
- **Look:** Harsh fluorescent white inside, dark blizzard outside, hanging carcasses, plastic strip curtains.
- **Boards / glass:** Stainless steel boards, meat hooks mounted on the walls.
- **Passive:** **Meat hooks** on walls — crush hits into them deal bleed damage.
- **Triggered:** **Blizzard gusts** from the open end — visibility drops, players slide.
- **Network Event — "Whiteout":** Full blizzard rolls in; visibility near zero except for flare items and jersey lights.
- **Crowd:** Plant workers in bloody aprons, fishermen, hunters.
- **Item delivery:** Rolled out of the cold-storage doors on carts.

### 7. Pier 13 — Bayonne Dockrats
- **Setting:** Rink built from stacked shipping containers at a working port.
- **Look:** Sodium lights, rain, wet steel, cargo cranes, ship horns.
- **Boards / glass:** Container walls; doors open and close.
- **Passive:** **Container doors** in the boards open randomly — shortcuts and ambush spots.
- **Triggered:** **Crane drop** — a shadow marks the ice, then a container slams down as a temporary obstacle.
- **Network Event — "Cargo Day":** Multiple containers drop, turning the rink into a maze for 30 s.
- **Crowd:** Dockworkers on stacked containers and crane cabs. Small, loud, loyal.
- **Item delivery:** Crane drops crates.

### 8. The Shaft — Sudbury Deep
- **Setting:** Underground rink carved into a nickel mine.
- **Look:** Very dark; headlamps on players' helmets, work lights, wet rock, dripping water.
- **Boards / glass:** Timber-braced rock walls, steel mesh.
- **Passive:** **Low light** — players are visible mostly by headlamps; you can turn yours off to sneak.
- **Triggered:** Dust & tremors — small rocks fall, minor damage, screen shake.
- **Network Event — "Cave-In":** Ceiling section collapses, rubble blocks part of the ice; players under it are crushed.
- **Crowd:** Miners on rock ledges and ore carts.
- **Item delivery:** Ore carts roll in on rails along the boards.

## Special Arenas

### The Pit — Practice / Tutorial
- Bare, abandoned community rink. Flickering fluorescents, graffiti, broken bleachers.
- No hazards. Used for Practice mode and as the **first vertical-slice arena** (cheapest to build, tests the rink core).

### Meridian Dome — Finals / Career Finale
- Home of the Meridian All-Stars. Massive, spotless TV studio arena — 360° LED screens, camera drones, pyrotechnics.
- **Passive:** Network cameras everywhere; the jumbotron shows live player "ratings."
- **Network Event — "Prime Time":** Lights cut, spotlights only on the top two players, the rest go dark — crowd votes on a "featured fight."
- Final boss arena for Career.

### Custom Arena (Creation Suite)
- Pick a base shell from any arena, mix hazards, set lighting/time of day/weather, crowd type, board sponsors, team colors.

## Build Plan

| Order | Arena | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Pit** | Vertical slice — tests rink core, cheap |
| 2 | **The Plant** (Detroit) | First hero arena; industrial look is easy with Megascans |
| 3 | **Pier 13** (Bayonne) | Career starts here (Dockrats) |
| 4–8 | Mill No. 9, The Yard, Kold Kut, The Barge, The Shaft | Remaining teams |
| 9 | Jackpot (Vegas) | Cleanest look — different material set |
| 10 | Meridian Dome | Career finale |

### Production approach ($0 pipeline)
- **One shared rink core** (ice, boards layout, nets, benches, penalty boxes) modeled in Blender — reused everywhere.
- **Modular themed kits** per arena: walls, stands, ceiling pieces, props — Blender + Megascans surfaces.
- **ComfyUI:** concept art for each arena first (mood/lighting target), then textures, signage, sponsor ads, posters, graffiti, jumbotron graphics.
- **Crowds:** instanced low-detail crowd characters (animated cards or simple meshes with variation) — must stay cheap for **split-screen**.
- **Performance target:** 60 fps in 2-player split-screen on the RTX 5080 at high settings.
