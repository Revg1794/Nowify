# RHL — Star Player Designs (Brainstorm v0.1)

Character sheets for every star skater and star goalie: look, gear, stats, fighting style, personality, voice, and
production notes. Builds on `LEAGUE_AND_NETWORK.md` (roster) and `GOALIE.md` (goalies).

---

## 1. Design Rules

1. **Readable silhouette.** Every star must be recognizable from the behind-the-player camera at full speed — by size, helmet/mask, and one standout gear piece.
2. **Grounded.** Real hockey gear, modified and abused: tape, rust, welds, blood, scratches. No sci-fi armor.
3. **Team colors first, personal details second.** Jersey reads as the team; scars, tattoos, gear mods read as the star.
4. **One signature prop or mark per star** (Kowalski's welding-scarred forearms, Hooks' meat hook, Canary's headlamp).
5. **Faces tell the story.** Scars, missing teeth, broken noses — the RHL is written on their faces.

## 5. Production Pipeline ($0)

1. **Concept art (ComfyUI):** front/side/back turnaround + face close-up per star, generated from these sheets. Owner approves the look.
2. **Face & body (MetaHuman Creator):** build each face/body from the concept (age, build, skin, hair, beard).
3. **Scars, tattoos, missing teeth:** texture/decal layers (ComfyUI-generated tattoo art, painted scar masks) + small mesh edits in Blender.
4. **Gear (Blender):** shared base kit (helmet, cage, visor, pads, gloves, skates, jersey) + per-star mods (melted visor, meat hook, headlamp mask, rosary, gold skates).
5. **Jerseys:** shared jersey mesh + team material (colors, logo, number, wear, blood).
6. **Goalie masks:** shared mask meshes (fiberglass, cage, modern) + painted designs (mask editor format so they double as presets).
7. **Animations:** shared hockey animation set + per-star walkout, celebration, signature move (Mixamo/Game Animation Sample base + Control Rig edits).
8. **Voices:** local TTS voice per star matching the voice notes; lines from `AUDIO.md` / `AI.md` §13.
9. **Review pipeline:** every star goes through the asset review (`GAME_DESIGN.md` §7): turntable, in-arena lighting, behind-the-player-cam readability test.

### Readability Test (per star)
- Recognizable from behind at full speed? (helmet/mask, size, signature piece)
- Distinct from teammates and other stars in silhouette?
- Team colors read clearly under the arena's lighting (and in Blackout/Whiteout)?

### Build Order
1. **Frankie Moretti** + **Tommy Russo** (Dockrats — Career start; first hero characters)
